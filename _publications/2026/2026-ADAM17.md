abstract: >-
  Biliary tract cancer (BTC) lacks effective, broad-spectrum therapeutic targets. To overcome the limitations of conventional target discovery, we developed ACT-Target, a modality-aware computational agent, to systematically identify therapeutic candidates and preclinically validate ADAM17-directed antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) and chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T cells.
